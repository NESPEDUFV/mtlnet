\chapter{Adaptation of the HGI Baseline}
\label{apx:hgi-tuning}

This appendix records one methodological choice that is necessary to interpret the
HGI baseline used in the later studies. Huang et al.\ set the cross-region edge weight
to 0.4 in the HGI experiments on Xiamen and Shenzhen~\cite{huang2023hgi}. The present
work did not transfer that value without evaluation. It selected the weight on Alabama
before applying HGI to the United States state graphs.

The sweep evaluated weights 0.4, 0.5, 0.6, and 0.7 with five folds and a budget of 50
epochs. The recorded category F1, on a zero-to-one scale and reported as the mean plus
or minus the standard deviation across folds, increased in the same order:

\begin{center}
\begin{tabular}{cc}
  \toprule
  Cross-region weight & Category F1 \\
  \midrule
  0.4 & $0.7388 \pm 0.0205$ \\
  0.5 & $0.7678 \pm 0.0211$ \\
  0.6 & $0.7944 \pm 0.0186$ \\
  0.7 & $0.8186 \pm 0.0123$ \\
  \bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{center}

The later studies therefore use 0.7. This sweep supports adaptation of one baseline
hyperparameter; it is not evidence about the relative performance of HGI and
Check2HGI, and it does not change HGI's role in the representation lineage.
