% CROSS-VOLUME REFERENCES -- the one mechanism in this file that needs explaining.
%
% THE PROBLEM. Both appendices below discuss chapters of the DISSERTATION, which is a
% different document. `\ref{ch:cbic}` therefore has no target here: compiled alone, every
% one of them renders as `??` and the build reports undefined references. There are 46 such
% call sites (measured: see the count in the gate named below), and rewriting all 46 by hand
% into literal numbers would put 46 unguarded numbers in prose, which is exactly the drift
% AGENT_HANDOFF §2.6 describes -- a chapter reorder would silently falsify every one.
%
% THE MECHANISM. \dissertationlabel declares one frozen (label -> number) pair, in the
% internal form \ref reads, so the existing `\ref{...}` call sites keep working unchanged and
% no appendix prose is edited. The number is FROZEN, so it must be guarded, and it is:
%   src_utils/check_extra_xrefs.py  reads the number the MAIN build actually printed for
%   each label out of build/main-aux/**/*.aux and fails if a frozen value here disagrees.
% It runs in src_utils/check.sh, so `make check` catches a stale value. It also fails when a
% \ref target used by this volume is not declared here at all, and when a declared label is
% no longer referenced -- the third direction matters, because a dead declaration is what
% makes the other two look green.
%
% THE VALUES BELOW WERE READ, NOT REMEMBERED. Each comes from the \newlabel line the
% defense build wrote, at commit 3bd47d5d:
%   grep -rh 'newlabel{ch:cbic}' build/main-aux/    ->  \newlabel{ch:cbic}{{3}{26}{...}}
% and likewise for the other eight. The first braced group is the printed number.
%
% THE LINK TARGET. A frozen label has no destination inside this volume, and pointing one at
% a destination that does not exist reproduces the dead-link defect this project has already
% had once (preamble.tex's hyperfootnotes note: ten footnote marks that all jumped to physical
% page 1). So every frozen reference is anchored at ext:dissertation, a real destination
% created in the opening statement below, where the sentence explaining that the dissertation
% is a separate document lives. A reader who clicks "Chapter 5" lands on that explanation
% rather than on nothing.
% ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
\makeatletter
\newcommand{\dissertationlabel}[2]{\@namedef{r@#1}{{#2}{1}{}{ext:dissertation}{}}}
\makeatother

    This volume holds five appendices that were
    written for the dissertation named on the
    cover of this volume and that are published beside it rather than inside it. The first
    records every departure of the three reproduced article chapters from their published or
    submitted originals. The second measures how much of the next category can be predicted from
    the category history of the input window alone, which is the reference level that one screening
    argument in the dissertation is read against. The third states the author's position on
    whether review by a research ethics committee was required for this work. The fourth records how
    one hyperparameter of the place-embedding baseline was selected before that baseline was applied
    to the datasets studied here. The fifth widens the dedicated next-category model until it carries
    the joint model's parameter budget, holding the representation, the folds, and the training
    protocol fixed, and finds the wider model scoring below the narrower one it replaces at the
    dataset where it ran the full four-seed protocol.

    The first two were part of the main document until July of 2026. They were moved here on the
    advisor's assessment that a reproduced chapter need not be an identical copy of the article
    it reproduces, and that the short preface opening each of those chapters already tells the
    reader what was changed and why. That assessment settles the question the errata appendix
    was written to answer, at least for the reader of the dissertation. It leaves the underlying
    record worth keeping, because a departure from a published text should be documented
    somewhere a reader can reach, and this volume is where it is documented.

    The last three left the main document in August of 2026, on the author's judgment that each
    answers a question a reader may bring to the work without being needed to follow its argument.
    They are published here for the same reason the first two are. A record that a reader can reach
    is worth more than a record that is left out because it did not fit the shape of the chapter it
    would have interrupted.

    \textbf{The dissertation is complete without this volume.} Nothing here is required to
    follow its argument, to read any of its results, or to judge the work: every claim the
    dissertation makes is stated and supported in the dissertation itself. What this volume adds
    is auditability. A reader comparing a reproduced chapter against the article it came from
    will find the differences enumerated here instead of having to reconstruct them, and a
    reader who wants the fuller treatment of the label-history benchmark will find it here
    rather than in the paragraph that summarizes it. A reader who asks how the human-subjects
    question was handled, or how the baseline was adapted before it
    was used will find each of those answered here as well.

    Two conventions follow from the split. The first two appendices keep the letters they carried
    when they were part of the dissertation, B and D, so that the records and correspondence that
    name them by letter still point at the same material, while the two that arrived later carry
    E and F, letters of this volume alone; the dissertation letters its own appendices
    independently, so an appendix letter should always be read together with the volume it belongs
    to. And a reference in
    these pages to a numbered chapter, section, table or figure is a reference into the
    dissertation, not into this volume: this volume numbers only its own five appendices and
    their tables.
