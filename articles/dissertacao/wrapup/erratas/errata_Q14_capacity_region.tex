\paragraph*{Errata: o limite de capacidade no eixo de regiao (Cap.~5, lista de limites).}
A lista passa a ter cinco membros. O quinto le:

\begin{quote}
Capacity on the region axis. The joint model has more parameters than the two dedicated models
together, so its region result at Texas and California may come from size rather than from sharing.
A control separates the two at California. It widens the dedicated region model until its parameter
count reaches the joint model's, taken from the training logs, and changes nothing else. At width
$352$ the dedicated model has $5{,}014{,}942$ parameters, $97.4$ percent of the joint model's
$5{,}151{,}189$, and scores $0.41$ Acc@10 above it, with all five folds in the same direction and a
paired test over the five folds that separates the difference from zero ($p = 0.010$). A wider arm,
at $174.8$ percent of the joint model's budget, scores $0.43$ above it, on the same test
($p = 0.008$, five of five folds); the two arms are $0.02$ apart ($p = 0.40$), so widening past
parity adds nothing. The widths of these arms were derived
against a reconstructed count of the joint model, not the measured one, which is why the arm at
parity is the narrower of the two. Texas has no capacity-matched control: the only arm run there has
$170.5$ percent of the joint model's budget and scores $0.21$ above the joint model, which the same
test does not separate from zero ($p = 0.12$). At California, the one dataset where the control
exists, the region advantage reported here therefore measures capacity, not transfer between the
tasks. What remains is the operational result: one model produces both predictions in one forward
pass, at no measured cost on either task. The category axis is not affected, because there a
dedicated model with $6.7$ times the parameters scores lower, not higher.
\end{quote}

\noindent
Esta errata enfraquece uma afirmacao do texto depositado. O custo de parametros ja aparece na
p.~73, e a p.~84 ja nao credita o resultado de regiao ao tronco compartilhado. O que faltava era o
limite nomeado e, agora, a medicao que o fecha.
