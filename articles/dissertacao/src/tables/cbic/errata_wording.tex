``Also, it is important to notice that since we have an unbalanced result for the MTL

Rewritten for readability, with the claim unchanged: the chapter states that the two

sentences describe, and ``the worse of other results'' had no grammatical referent. The
hedge ``could'' is preserved, no number is touched, and no inferential claim is added. \\
