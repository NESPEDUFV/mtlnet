## coletanea_rules

From the live Normas gerais (rev. 11/10/2019), read in full (verbatim Portuguese):
- §2.3: body may be organized as (i) texto corrido, (ii) capítulos, or (iii) "artigos científicos pertinentes ao trabalho de conclusão, publicados, aceitos, ou submetidos para publicação" (so submitted-only articles ARE eligible); mixed form capítulos+artigos admitted.
- §2.6: artigos-format body = "(i) Introdução Geral, (ii) Artigo(s) Científico(s) e (iii) Conclusão Geral. A Introdução Geral e as Conclusões Gerais poderão conter suas respectivas bibliografias. Os artigos terão formatação livre, desde que seja adotada uma consistência interna."
- §2.6.1: single-article dissertation → Introdução Geral and Conclusões Gerais are WAIVED ("serão dispensadas").
- §2.6.2: articles with DIFFERENT formattings admitted in the same work.
- §2.6.3: articles in DIFFERENT LANGUAGES admitted in the same work (on top of §1.3: overall work in PT, EN or ES at Comissão Orientadora's discretion).
- §2.6.4: previously PUBLISHED article(s) may be reproduced from the originals, provided item 3 (Editoração: legible permanent print, black high-definition characters, SI units, A4 suggested, binding margin >=40 mm others >=20 mm, pre-textual pages counted not numbered) is respected.
- §2.7: optional closing sections such as "Avanços Esperados, Perspectivas Futuras e outras" may be added under any format.
- §2.1–2.2: mandatory frame: capa (UFV), pre-textual pages (rosto with ficha catalográfica on verso; approval page with dates/signatures; optional dedicatória/agradecimentos/biografia/lista de símbolos; Resumo PT + Abstract EN with the prescribed header; Sumário), corpo, optional anexos.
PPGCC-SPECIFIC ADDITIONS: none on format — the new regimento interno (09/07/2026) does not regulate dissertation format/coletânea at all; PPGCC adds procedural layers only: NBR 14724/2011 + NBR 6023/2018 compliance, its pre-textual formatting checklist, the Word PT/EN models, anti-plagiarism certificate, and the Art. 21/22 defense prerequisites.

## ai_policy_findings

NO BINDING UFV OR PPGCC RULE FOUND. Searched UFV-wide and PPGCC pages: no resolução/portaria on AI-assisted writing or mandatory AI disclosure in theses. The only UFV-affiliated document is "Diretrizes essenciais para declaração do uso de IA em pesquisas acadêmicas" (Bethania Medeiros Geremias, DPE/UFV, 05/03/2026, https://tecido.dpe.ufv.br/wp-content/uploads/2026/03/IA-em-Pesquisa-Educacional_-Recomendacoes-1.pdf) — read in full: an individual faculty pedagogical resource (explicitly "recurso pedagógico online", based on ANPAD/COPE/Elsevier/Ipea/USP guidelines), NOT a norm; it recommends a dedicated "Uso de IAG nesta Pesquisa" section, mandatory declaration for substantial editing/content-generation/data-analysis/image-generation uses (with suggested boilerplate), and flags AI authorship or fabricated data as misconduct. PPGCC's own hard requirement adjacent to this: the defense checklist makes an ANTI-PLAGIARISM certificate (UFV's institutional tools) mandatory — "A defesa não será aprovada caso esse certificado não seja entregue."
NATIONAL LEVEL (verified): CNPq Portaria nº 2.664, 06/03/2026, institutes the Política de Integridade na Atividade Científica — first national guidelines on generative AI in research: any IAG use must be declared "qualquer que seja o tipo de IAG e a fase do desenvolvimento da pesquisa, especificando a ferramenta utilizada e a finalidade"; "vedada a submissão de conteúdo gerado por IAG como se fosse de autoria humana"; authors retain full responsibility; creates the CIAC integrity commission; applies to CNPq-linked servidores/proponentes/beneficiários/bolsistas (confirmed at https://www.gov.br/cnpq/pt-br/assuntos/noticias/cnpq-em-acao/cnpq-publica-portaria-que-institui-politica-de-integridade-na-atividade-cientifica; full text at memoria2.cnpq.br, cert error blocked direct fetch). It does not specifically mention dissertations, but IF the student is a CNPq (or, per secondary reports, CAPES) scholarship holder it effectively mandates AI-use declaration. CAPES: no portaria specific to theses as of mid-2026; verified primary artifact is the April/2025 GT report "A inteligência artificial na pesquisa e no fomento" (https://www.gov.br/capes/pt-br/centrais-de-conteudo/23042025_Relatorio_2575649_A_inteligencia_artificial_na_pesquisa_e_no_fomento.pdf); secondary sources cite a CAPES Nota Técnica nº 3/2025 and Ofício-Circular nº 5/2026/DIRAV/CAPES telling programs to adopt the CNPq portaria as reference — primary texts NOT located (see open_questions). PRACTICAL IMPLICATION for the August 2026 defense: no UFV rule forbids AI-assisted writing, but a short AI-use disclosure note in the dissertation is the defensible move, aligned with CNPq 2.664/2026 (esp. if bolsista) and the UFV/DPE recommendation format.
