# Brief: Simulating a Brazilian CS Master's Banca (coletânea de artigos format)

## 1. How the banca actually works (mechanics to build into the persona)

- **Composition**: advisor (president, usually doesn't argue) + 2 examiners + suplente, all doctors, ≥1 external to the institution ([UFU PPGCO](https://ppgco.facom.ufu.br/servicos/defesas-de-dissertacao-qualificacao-e-tese)).
- **Format**: public session; presentation up to **50 min** (UFV PPGCC Regimento Art. 23 says "seminário com duração máxima de 50 minutos"); then **arguição** — each member gets ~15–30 min of questions, candidate gets equal time to reply; then closed deliberation (10–30 min) and result announcement ([UFV PPGCC regimento](https://ppgcc.ufv.br/regimento-interno/), [UFU](https://ppgco.facom.ufu.br/servicos/defesas-de-dissertacao-qualificacao-e-tese), [Dra. Nathalia — Defesa de Mestrado](https://www.doutoranathalia.com.br/blog/defesa-de-mestrado)).
- **Crucial dynamic**: the **written dissertation, pre-read, determines the result — not the talk**. Members arrive with formed opinions and a list of annotations; the arguição tests whether the candidate can defend and explain what's written ([Dra. Nathalia](https://www.doutoranathalia.com.br/blog/defesa-de-mestrado)).
- Many arguição turns are **disguised correction requests** ("não acha que X fortaleceria o trabalho?") — the persona should mix genuine questions with suggestions it expects to see in the corrected version ([Dra. Nathalia](https://www.doutoranathalia.com.br/blog/defesa-de-mestrado)).
- **Examiners want to pass you.** Research on examiner behavior (30-study systematic review): examiners expect and want a pass, read like normal readers + academics, form first impressions by end of ch. 2–3 (usually the lit review), and "flip" into hypercritical mode when they hit sloppiness — typos, careless errors, incoherence erode "intellectual confidence" ([Golding et al., What examiners do](https://www.tandfonline.com/doi/abs/10.1080/02602938.2013.859230); [Mullins & Kiley, "It's a PhD, not a Nobel Prize"](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/228816715_'It's_a_PhD_not_a_Nobel_Prize'_How_experienced_examiners_assess_research_theses); [summary](https://www.eliteediting.com.au/know-your-enemy-understanding-thesis-examiners/)).

## 2. Evaluation dimensions (what members score, explicitly or implicitly)

Composite of Brazilian guidance + international rubrics ([Dra. Nathalia — pode reprovar?](https://www.doutoranathalia.com.br/blog/pode-reprovar-na-defesa-de-mestrado/), [UTwente MSc rubric](https://www.utwente.nl/en/est/masterest/final-project-trajectory-started-in-2025-2026/assessment-form-and-rubric-final-project-est.pdf), [Univ. Arizona defense eval](https://mas.arizona.edu/sites/mas.arizona.edu/files/2024-05/MAS%20Graduate%20Thesis%20Defense%20Eval.pdf), [Andrews rubric](https://www.andrews.edu/grad/au-dissertation-defense-evaluation-rubric-current.pdf)):

1. **Problem clarity & delimitation** — is the research question sharp and answerable; are objectives met by the end?
2. **Command of the state of the art** — lit review current, critical (not catalog), work positioned against key competitors.
3. **Methodological coherence** — methods suit the objectives; choices justified against alternatives (not "my advisor suggested it").
4. **Rigor & honesty of results** — fair baselines, sound splits/statistics, no overclaiming; claims calibrated to evidence.
5. **Contribution** — a nameable, defendable delta over prior work (master's standard: competent original work, not Nobel).
6. **Recognition of limitations** — banca *knows* every work has them; it tests whether the candidate does too.
7. **Candidate's ownership** — can they explain every decision, every number, every figure without notes; do they clearly own the work vs advisor/co-authors?
8. **Text quality** — organization, writing, consistency; sloppiness is punished disproportionately (the "flip").
9. **(Coletânea only) Unity of the whole** — see §4.
10. **Defense performance** — clarity, timing discipline, composure, and quality of answers under pressure (secondary to the text but scored in rubrics).

## 3. Coletânea de artigos — what "properly unified" means to a committee

**UFV's own rule (governs a UFV PPGCC dissertation)**: the corpo may be "texto corrido", "capítulos", or "artigos científicos" (published, accepted, **or submitted**); the article format **must** be *(i) Introdução Geral, (ii) Artigo(s), (iii) Conclusão Geral* (frame chapters waived only for a single-article dissertation); mixed chapters+articles allowed; articles may keep different formats/languages if internally consistent; previously published articles may be reproduced verbatim; optional closing sections (Perspectivas Futuras etc.) allowed; the banca is explicitly empowered to demand changes in "forma, linguagem e conteúdo" ([UFV Normas Gerais de Teses e Dissertações, rev. 2019](https://www.ppg.ufv.br/wp-content/uploads/2012/08/Normas-gerais-de-Teses-e-Dissertac%CC%A7o%CC%83es-12.pdf), items 1.2, 2.3–2.7). UFV PPGCC additionally requires, before the defense, proof of ≥1 paper published/accepted at an event or submitted to a qualifying journal, and delivery of the text ≥20 days ahead ([regimento](https://ppgcc.ufv.br/regimento-interno/)).

Comparable CS-program rules confirm the pattern: Unicamp IC's "formato alternativo" mandates Introdução + papers + **Discussão (mandatory with 2+ papers)** + Conclusão + Referências, with publisher authorizations annexed ([IC Unicamp](https://ic.unicamp.br/pos-graduacao/informacao-para-docentes-e-pos-graduandos/vida-academica-da-pos-graduacao/formato-alternativo-de-tese/)); UFPE relegates non-first-author papers to annexes ([UFPE norms](https://www.ufpe.br/documents/40006/0/Normas+de+apresenta%C3%A7%C3%A3o+para+qualifica%C3%A7%C3%A3o-defesa+dos+projetos+de+Disserta%C3%A7%C3%A3o+e+Tese.pdf/6b8870c7-d736-467f-a24a-d6ba066fa38a)).

**What the committee actually checks for unification** (Brazilian framing + PBT-examiner research):

- A **fio condutor**: the Introdução Geral must state the *rationale* that makes the papers one investigation, otherwise the work reads as a "colcha de retalhos" (patchwork) — the classic dismissal ([Marco Mello — Sobrevivendo na Ciência](https://marcoarmello.wordpress.com/2014/03/15/dissertacao/)).
- The frame chapters must argue at **thesis level, not paper level**: examiners reward integrative chapters that read as one sustained argument and punish "Paper 1 / Paper 2" recaps, restated abstracts, and limitations discussed only inside each paper ([thesify — integrative discussion chapter](https://www.thesify.ai/blog/phd-by-publication-discussion-chapter)).
- **Inconsistencies between papers must be confronted, not hidden**: differing results/setups across papers should be interpreted (boundary conditions, corrected methodology, evolved understanding). An honest account of *what changed between paper 1 and paper 2 and why* reads as scientific maturity; silence reads as incoherence ([thesify](https://www.thesify.ai/blog/phd-by-publication-discussion-chapter)).
- **Individual contribution in co-authored papers** is a documented examiner concern for publication-based theses, along with whole-thesis coherence; ~29% of examiners apply their *own* extra criteria to this format ([Sharmini et al., Assessing the doctoral thesis when it includes published work](https://www.tandfonline.com/doi/abs/10.1080/02602938.2014.888535)).
- Peer-reviewed acceptance of the papers does **not** immunize them — the banca can still demand content changes in the dissertation version (UFV item 1.2), and examiners report critiquing published chapters anyway.
- Surface unification the banca notices immediately: consistent notation/terminology across papers, cross-references between chapters, non-redundant introductions (the per-paper intros vs the Introdução Geral), one unified conclusions chapter that answers the dissertation-level question.

## 4. Outcomes, major corrections, failure

- Outcomes in practice: **aprovado sem ressalvas** (uncommon), **aprovado com correções** (menores/substanciais — the modal outcome), **reprovado** (rare). Corrections due typically **30–90 days** (some programs up to 6 months for substantial ones), validated by the advisor or, for substantial changes, by banca members ([Dra. Nathalia — aprovado com ressalvas](https://www.doutoranathalia.com.br/blog/parecer-da-banca-aprovado-com-ressalvas---e-agora), [pode reprovar?](https://www.doutoranathalia.com.br/blog/pode-reprovar-na-defesa-de-mestrado/)).
- **Triggers for reprovação** (extreme cases): substantial plagiarism; radical, unjustified divergence from the qualified project; no real contribution (mere compilation); fundamental methodological flaws that invalidate the conclusions ([Dra. Nathalia](https://www.doutoranathalia.com.br/blog/pode-reprovar-na-defesa-de-mestrado/)). Failure also embarrasses the advisor — the system filters before the defense.
- **Triggers for correções substanciais** (what a simulator should threaten): weak/outdated related-work chapter; unjustified methodological choices; unsupported claims ("prove/demonstrate" without evidence); missing statistical treatment; frame chapters that don't integrate the papers; overclaimed generalization; and — for ML work specifically — unfair baselines or suspected leakage.

## 5. Realistic question bank — MTL / POI-prediction dissertation (grouped; each tests something specific)

The generic core (questions 1–7 below cover ~80% of any arguição per [tesify's defense-question guide](https://tesify.pt/defesa-tese-perguntas-preparacao-2025/); the rest are the CS/ML and coletânea-specific patterns a real banca adds).

**Contribution & positioning**
1. "Em uma frase: qual é a contribuição original desta dissertação — da dissertação, não de cada artigo?" (tests thesis-level "so what")
2. "O que seu trabalho mostra que [key related work, e.g., a strong single-task next-POI model or an existing MTL mobility paper] ainda não mostrava?" (state-of-the-art command)
3. "Se a comunidade só pudesse lembrar de um resultado seu, qual deveria ser, e por quê esse resultado é confiável?"

**Method justification**
4. "Por que multi-task learning? O que o senhor perde treinando dois modelos separados — e o senhor *mediu* essa comparação com o mesmo orçamento de tuning para os dois lados?" (fair-baseline probe)
5. "Por que essas duas tarefas juntas (categoria + próximo POI)? Como o senhor sabe que há transferência positiva e não apenas regularização? Observou transferência negativa em algum estado/dataset?"
6. "Por que esse mecanismo de balanceamento de perdas e não alternativas mais simples? Uma soma ponderada estática não bastaria?" (tests whether complexity is earned — dangerous if a static weight actually won)
7. "Justifique a escolha dos embeddings/substrato. Que alternativas considerou e por que foram descartadas?"

**Experimental rigor** (where ML bancas are hardest)
8. "Como o senhor garantiu que não há vazamento entre treino e validação — em particular, informação do futuro do usuário, ou estruturas globais (grafos, matrizes de transição) construídas com dados de teste?" (leakage — the classic kill-shot)
9. "Por que Acc@k e macro-F1? O que essas métricas escondem? Por que não MRR/NDCG, e o que muda no ranking dos métodos se mudar a métrica?"
10. "Seus ganhos são estatisticamente significativos? Quantas seeds, quantos folds, qual teste, e a variância entre folds é maior ou menor que o ganho reportado?"
11. "Os baselines foram tunados com o mesmo esforço que o seu método? Como o leitor sabe que não é uma comparação enviesada?"
12. "Há um ablation que isole cada componente da arquitetura? Qual componente, removido, menos afeta o resultado — e por que ele está no modelo então?"
13. "Por que esses datasets/regiões? Dados de check-in de rede social representam mobilidade real? O que acontece com usuários e POIs raros (cold-start)?"
14. "Seus resultados generalizam para outra cidade/país, ou o senhor treinou e avaliou no mesmo universo? Qual é o argumento de validade externa?"

**Limitations & reflection**
15. "Quais são as três principais limitações do trabalho — e qual delas o senhor considera a mais grave para as conclusões?" (never answer "nenhuma")
16. "Se recomeçasse hoje, o que faria diferente — metodologicamente e no processo?"
17. "Quais as implicações práticas? Quem usaria isso, e o custo computacional torna o uso viável?"
18. "Dados de mobilidade individual são sensíveis. Que considerações éticas/de privacidade se aplicam, e o que o senhor fez a respeito?"

**Coletânea-specific**
19. "Qual é o fio condutor entre os artigos? Convença-me de que isto é uma dissertação, e não dois artigos grampeados." (the format's central test)
20. "O que o artigo 2 corrige, contradiz ou refina do artigo 1? Os números divergem entre eles — qual versão devo acreditar, e por que a Conclusão Geral não discute essa evolução?" (honest-arc probe)
21. "Nos artigos em coautoria, o que exatamente foi contribuição sua, e o que foi dos coautores/orientador?" (documented examiner concern for this format — [Sharmini et al.](https://www.tandfonline.com/doi/abs/10.1080/02602938.2014.888535))
22. "A notação/terminologia muda entre os capítulos [aponta exemplo]. A Introdução Geral repete as introduções dos artigos. Por que devo aceitar isso como um texto unificado?"
23. "A Conclusão Geral responde qual pergunta? Ela apenas resume os artigos ou afirma algo que nenhum artigo sozinho afirma?"
24. "O que ficou de fora dos artigos — experimentos negativos, tentativas falhas — e por que isso não está documentado na dissertação?" (tests honesty; strong candidates own their negative results)
25. "Como trabalho futuro, o que o senhor faria primeiro, e essa pergunta surgiu de qual resultado específico?"

## 6. Red flags the simulator should punish

- Can't explain a choice beyond "foi o que o orientador sugeriu" / "é o que a literatura usa" ([Dra. Nathalia](https://www.doutoranathalia.com.br/blog/defesa-de-mestrado)).
- Claims of no limitations, or defensiveness when limitations are pressed (examiners push until admitted; [tesify](https://tesify.pt/defesa-tese-perguntas-preparacao-2025/)).
- Improvising answers without foundation instead of an honest "isso minha pesquisa não explorou; é uma direção futura relevante".
- Numbers in the talk that differ from the text; unfamiliarity with own tables/figures.
- Frame chapters that only summarize papers (paper-by-paper recap = the top integrative-chapter failure; [thesify](https://www.thesify.ai/blog/phd-by-publication-discussion-chapter)).
- Ignored inter-paper contradictions; inconsistent notation; redundant intros.
- Overclaiming ("provamos", "estado da arte") beyond the evidence; unfair or missing baselines; no significance treatment.
- Sloppy text — triggers the documented hypercritical "flip" ([Mullins & Kiley](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/228816715_'It's_a_PhD_not_a_Nobel_Prize'_How_experienced_examiners_assess_research_theses)).
- Blown timing (going far over the presentation slot).

## 7. What impresses a banca

- A crisp, ownable one-sentence contribution, stated early and repeated in the Conclusão Geral.
- Considered-and-rejected alternatives narrated for every major design choice (method, metric, dataset).
- Limitations volunteered before being asked, each with a mitigation and a consequence for the conclusions.
- An honest evolution narrative across the papers ("o artigo 2 revisita X porque descobrimos Y") — turns the coletânea's weakness into evidence of scientific process.
- Calibrated claims + per-fold/multi-seed statistics; knowing exactly where a number comes from.
- Answering the hard question by *conceding the valid part* and defending the rest with evidence — never total defensiveness, never total capitulation ([Dra. Nathalia](https://www.doutoranathalia.com.br/blog/defesa-de-mestrado)).
- Distinção mention exists in some programs for unanimously extraordinary work+defense ([UniCEUB criteria](https://www.uniceub.br/arquivo/83ng_20180925120450*pdf?AID=2112)).

## 8. Borrowable non-Brazil rubrics (for scoring scales in the persona)

- [UTwente MSc final-project rubric](https://www.utwente.nl/en/est/masterest/final-project-trajectory-started-in-2025-2026/assessment-form-and-rubric-final-project-est.pdf) — dimension×level grid incl. RQ clarity, method suitability, analysis, defense.
- [Univ. of Arizona thesis-defense evaluation](https://mas.arizona.edu/sites/mas.arizona.edu/files/2024-05/MAS%20Graduate%20Thesis%20Defense%20Eval.pdf) and [Andrews Univ. rubric](https://www.andrews.edu/grad/au-dissertation-defense-evaluation-rubric-current.pdf) — separate scores for written work, oral presentation, and response-to-questions ("masterfully/competently/adequately defends").
- [URI defense rubric](https://web.uri.edu/wp-content/uploads/sites/1970/Thesis-dissertation_defense_evaluation_2013C1-1.doc), [UMN-Duluth MS proposal rubric](https://scse.d.umn.edu/sites/scse.d.umn.edu/files/dees-rubric_for_evaluation_of_ms_thesis_proposal_pdf_new_5-24-19.pdf).
- [QUT thesis-by-publication guidelines](https://cms.qut.edu.au/__data/assets/pdf_file/0004/451903/faculty-of-education-thesis-by-publication-guidelines.pdf) — examiners must weigh "coherence of the thesis as a whole and the way each paper contributes to it".

**Persona synthesis hint**: model each member as (a) pre-read the text with an annotation list; (b) opens with a courtesy compliment, then works through annotations mixing genuine probes (§5) and disguised correction requests; (c) escalates on evasiveness, sloppiness, or overclaiming; (d) for a coletânea, at least one member dedicates their turn to Q19–Q23; (e) verdict defaults to "aprovado com correções" with a written corrections list — reserving "correções substanciais" for §4 triggers.
