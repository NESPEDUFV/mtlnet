## Sanity rule for the drafting agent

If a Ch.1 sentence is defending a choice rather than stating it, it belongs in §2.1, a preface, a
recap, or Ch.6 — move it. The Introduction earns the reader's trust by narrating confidently, not by
answering objections the reader has not yet formed.
