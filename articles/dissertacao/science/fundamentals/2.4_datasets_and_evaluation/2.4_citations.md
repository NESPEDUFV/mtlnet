## 2.4  Datasets and evaluation

_Argument order: the datasets of record -> the metrics and the baselines they are read against -> the validation protocol (user-disjoint cross-validation) -> the significance machinery (paired test -> correction -> non-inferiority)._

| # | Cite (key) | Sentence it supports |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | `cho2011gowalla` | Gowalla is the check-in dataset of record, introduced with the study of periodic and social movement in LBSNs. **(dataset origin)** |
| 2 | `[new] yang2015tsmc` | The Foursquare NYC/Tokyo check-in dataset (TSMC2014) is the other standard benchmark, distinct from the Cultural-Mapping release. **(dataset origin; kept distinct)** |
| 3 | `wongso2025massivesteps` | Massive-STEPS supplies the Istanbul benchmark and argues the field over-relies on decade-old datasets, motivating a fresh multi-city evaluation. `[preprint arXiv:2505.11239]` |
| 4 | `[new] sokolova2009measures` | Macro-averaged F1 averages per-class F-measure and is the appropriate primary metric under class imbalance, as in next **category**. **(defines macro-F1)** |
| 5 | `[new] gambs2012mmc` | A mobility Markov chain predicts the next **place** from transition history and serves as the non-learned baseline floor. **(defines the Markov baseline; targets next place)** |
| 6 | `song2010limits` | The predictability ceiling gives the upper reference point a learned model is read against; the paper reports a 93% potential predictability across the user base (verified firsthand from the PDF). |
| 7 | `[new] kohavi1995crossval` | Stratified k-fold cross-validation is the recommended protocol for accuracy estimation and model selection. **(defines k-fold CV)** |
| 8 | `[new] pedregosa2011sklearn` | The grouped, user-disjoint splitter (StratifiedGroupKFold) prevents a user's check-ins from leaking across folds. | 10 | `holm1979` | The Holm step-down procedure controls family-wise error under multiple comparisons. **(defines the correction)** |
| 11 | `lakens2017tost` | Two one-sided tests (TOST) establish statistical equivalence within a pre-set margin, the basis for a non-inferiority ('matches') claim. **(defines TOST; 'matches' is bound to it)** |

_Verb-to-test binding (writing law): 'outperforms' is licensed only by the paired Wilcoxon superiority test `[new] wilcoxon1945` (with Holm `holm1979`); 'matches' only by TOST non-inferiority `lakens2017tost` within the two-point margin.
