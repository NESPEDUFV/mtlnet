## 2.1  POI-prediction tasks

_Goal: establish the task space and separate the three prediction targets the dissertation keeps distinct, then situate the sequence-model lineage that the field built around next **place**._

| # | Cite (key) | Sentence it supports |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | `song2010limits` | Human mobility is highly regular and carries a theoretical predictability ceiling, which motivates learned prediction of where a person goes next. |
| 2 | `luca2021mobilitysurvey` | Deep-learning approaches to human mobility span several distinct tasks, among them next-location and crowd-flow prediction, so the prediction target must be named precisely. **(defines the task family)** |
| 3 | `cho2011gowalla` | Movement in location-based social networks combines short-range periodic travel with socially driven long-range travel, which is the check-in behavior all these tasks model. **(defines LBSN / check-in)** |
| 4 | `liu2016strnn` | Next-**place** prediction is framed as a sequential problem, and ST-RNN extends the recurrent network with time- and distance-specific transition matrices. **(defines next place; opens the sequence lineage)** |
| 5 | `feng2018deepmove` | DeepMove predicts the next **place** from long, sparse trajectories with an attentional recurrent network over periodic mobility. |
| 6 | `[new] kong2018hstlstm` | HST-LSTM injects spatial-temporal transition context into a hierarchical LSTM for next-**place** (location) prediction. |
| 7 | `yang2020flashback` | Flashback predicts the next **place** by attending back over past hidden states to cope with trajectory sparsity. |
| 8 | `luo2021stan` | STAN shows non-adjacent visits carry predictive signal, using spatio-temporal attention for next-**place** recommendation. |
| 9 | `[new] lian2020geosan` | GeoSAN adds geography-aware self-attention with a hierarchical grid encoding for next-**place** recommendation. |
| 10 | `yang2022getnext` | GETNext injects a global trajectory-flow map into a transformer to bring collaborative signal into next-**place** prediction. |
| 11 | `lin2021ctle` | A place has different functions in different contexts, so CTLE learns context- and time-aware location embeddings per visit, the closest prior work to a per-visit view of place. → bridges to 2.2 |
| 12 | `Xu2023` | Static **category classification** (semantic venue annotation) is a separate, non-sequential task that labels a POI with a semantic category. **(defines category classification)** |
| 13 | `zhu2022drrgnn` | Predicting a person's next activity **region** is itself a studied target at the region level of granularity. **(defines next region)** |
| 14 | `Lim2022` | HMT-GRN predicts next **place** and next **region** jointly in a task hierarchy, using region prediction to constrain a beam search over places, an example where region is auxiliary to place rather than a co-equal end target. |
| 15 | `yu2020catdm` | Category-aware next-POI models (CatDM) use predicted **category** as an intermediate signal to prune place candidates, so here category is auxiliary, not an end target. **(distinguishes auxiliary vs end-target)** |
| 16 | `capanema2023poirgnn` | POI-RGNN predicts the **category** of the next place from recurrent and graph signals, an instance where next category is itself the end target. |

_Note: the dissertation predicts next **category** and next **region** and explicitly does **not** predict the exact next **place**; 2.1 states this once, here, after the three targets are defined._


### Notes specific to 2.1
- The three prediction targets are introduced here and never conflated afterward. State once, here, that the
  dissertation predicts next **category** and next **region** and does **not** predict the exact next **place**.
- The sequence-model lineage (ST-RNN -> DeepMove -> Flashback -> HST-LSTM -> STAN/GeoSAN/GETNext) is next-**place**
  work; it is background, not the dissertation's target. Keep it framed that way.
- `lin2021ctle` (CTLE) appears here as a per-visit location embedding and is **pulled forward to 2.2** as the
  static->contextual hinge (see 2.2, and GAP_STATUS gap #4).
- Massive-STEPS benchmark roster (FPMC, RNN, LSTPM, DeepMove, STAN, GETNext, STHGCN, TGAT, MobGT, ROTAN) is a
  next-**place** recommendation suite. The classic members are already anchored above; the graph-transformer /
  hypergraph members (STHGCN, TGAT, ROTAN) are frontier and belong in the paper chapters' related-work, not here.
