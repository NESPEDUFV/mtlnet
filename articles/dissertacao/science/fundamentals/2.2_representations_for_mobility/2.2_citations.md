### Notes specific to 2.2 (the chapter's spine)
- The pivot claim of the whole dissertation lives here: **a single static per-place vector cannot represent a
  visit** because a place has different functions in different contexts. It is anchored by `lin2021ctle` (CTLE),
  pulled forward from 2.1. This is the "static -> contextual" evidence (GAP_STATUS gap #4 -- addressed).
- HGI (`huang2023hgi`) is the direct base of the check-in-level representation. Its central claim -- training by
  **maximizing mutual information across the POI-region-city hierarchy** -- was confirmed firsthand this session
  from the downloaded PDF (../_verification/VERIFICATION_NOTES.md). No longer a [VERIFY].
- Time2Vec, SIREN, Space2Vec, Sphere2Vec, and FiLM enter as **general** representation methods cited at their own
  source of record (they originate outside mobility), per the author's Theme-B instruction.
- Lineage for the model-lineage table:
  distributed embeddings -> graph embeddings (GCN/GAT/GraphSAGE/node2vec/DeepWalk) -> MI objective (MINE/DIM) ->
  **DGI** -> **HGI** -> **check-in level / Check2HGI**.
